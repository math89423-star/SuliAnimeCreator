# H3 成人向 I2VA 范例全文（四档 · 5 秒单镜）

> 用法：照抄结构与句式，把人物/场景/道具换成自己的成年虚构设定。
> 对齐行、字段名、时间记号不许改（格式归 `video/h3-prompt-writing`）。
> 每例都是 **5 秒单镜、一镜一事件**——加事件请加镜，不要加词。

---

## T1 `tease` 氛围暗示 · 单人 · 窗边

```text
For the target video, at 0.00 seconds into the target video, <Picture 1> (from [Shot 1]) is fully referenced.

integrated_multimodal_description: [Shot 1] 2D-animated, adult woman, mature, a medium close-up frames the woman shown in <Picture 1>, preserving her hair, eye color, earrings, her damp skin along the collarbone, and the bedroom layout established by <Picture 1>. The camera pushes in with small amplitude at slow speed as her chest rises and falls with a single slow breath; her lips part slightly, her fingers curl against the sheet edge, and a few strands of hair shift across her cheek. Her gaze stays on the person just outside the frame.

overall_soundscape: A quiet bedroom with faint curtain movement and the soft rustle of the sheet under her hand. Her slow breathing is the clearest sound, ending on a small exhale.

non_diegetic_music: N/A
```

**要点**：水位停在肩颈线与呼吸；一个事件（呼吸）+ 三个微动作；画外人物只用"视线落在画外"交代，不写进来。

## T2 `undress` 半露 · 单人 · 遮挡三件套

```text
For the target video, at 0.00 seconds into the target video, <Picture 1> (from [Shot 1]) is fully referenced.

integrated_multimodal_description: [Shot 1] 2D-animated, adult woman, mature, a medium shot frames the woman in <Picture 1>, keeping her hair, her headpiece, the white thigh-highs, and the room layout intact. She is partially undressed: the blouse is open at the collar and has slipped off her right shoulder, and her forearm rests across her chest, her upper arm covering the rest, with the sheet gathered at her waist. The camera trucks right with small amplitude at slow speed as the loosened fabric slides one finger-width further down her arm, her shoulder blade rises with a shallow breath, and her hand closes slowly on the fabric without pulling it back up.

overall_soundscape: Fabric shifts with a soft drag against skin while the sheet whispers under her knee. Close, slow breathing continues beneath a low room tone.

non_diegetic_music: Sparse piano notes at a slow tempo, widely spaced, fading out before the end.
```

**要点**：遮挡三件套（前臂挡胸口 + 上臂覆盖其余 + 被单收在腰）；肢体只允许"滑落一寸"级别的关节动作；关键锚（发饰、白袜、被单）全程 carry。

## T3 `explicit` 直白 · 双人 · 体位先锁后动

```text
For the target video, at 0.00 seconds into the target video, <Picture 1> (from [Shot 1]) is fully referenced.

integrated_multimodal_description: [Shot 1] 2D-animated, adult woman and adult man, mature, both fully adult and mutually responsive, a medium shot frames the two shown in <Picture 1>, preserving her hair, her headpiece, the sailor-style blouse pushed up at the chest, the white thigh-highs, the drifting petals, and the bed layout established by <Picture 1>. He is positioned above and slightly to her right; his left hand is braced on the mattress beside her shoulder, his right hand holds her thigh, and her right calf is hooked over his lower back. The camera holds a static shot as their bodies stay pressed flush and move together in a single slow rhythm, skin compressing where they meet; her head tips back a fraction, her fingers tighten on his forearm, and her knee presses into his side. Toward the end the rhythm shortens and her back arches slightly.

overall_soundscape: Close breathing overlaps in a slow rhythm, joined by fabric drag against the sheet, a faint creak from the bed frame, and the soft sound of skin contact.

non_diegetic_music: A low sustained string tone at a slow tempo with a single piano tone near the end.
```

**要点**：锁位句在前（谁在哪 + 哪只手撑 + 哪条腿钩），节奏句在后；全程小幅度；交合面用"skin compressing where they meet"这类可见物理词；身份锚（发饰 / 白袜 / 花瓣）写进运动句 carry。

## T4 `closeup` 极致特写 · 局部 + 画外暗示

```text
For the target video, at 0.00 seconds into the target video, <Picture 1> (from [Shot 1]) is fully referenced.

integrated_multimodal_description: [Shot 1] 2D-animated, adult woman and adult man, mature, both fully adult and mutually responsive, an extreme close-up holds on the junction of their bodies below the waist, cropping both faces and torsos out of frame; the frame keeps the contact surface, her thigh, his forearm, and the gathered sheet from <Picture 1>. The camera pushes in with small amplitude at slow speed as the contact surface shifts by a fraction with each slow rhythm, a fine sheen of sweat slides along her inner thigh, and his fingers press deeper into her skin. The motion never leaves the crop.

overall_soundscape: Damp skin contact sounds in a slow rhythm with a faint bed creak beneath, and short breathing kept just behind them.

non_diegetic_music: N/A
```

**要点**：裁切控制水位（脸与躯干都出框，只在框内写可见物）；单点微动为主（光泽位移、手指下陷）；画外信息一律"不发生"。

---

## 换档口诀（回退 / 分流时用）

同一镜换档**只动三处**：① 水位词（`partial undress` ↔ `pressed flush`）② 遮挡结构（加减前臂/被单/花瓣）③ 动作幅度（`a single slow rhythm` ↔ `a single slow breath`）。
**不动**：对齐行、字段顺序、时长、运镜、锚点 carry 清单——这样镜表与交付包结构不变，便于对照验收。
